    How it works

    Your kernel runs on a simulated GPU. Each warp of 32 threads executes one instruction at a time for all its active lanes. When lanes disagree at a branch the warp runs both sides, one after the other, and joins them again where the paths meet.

    Scores: cycles is the total over all warps (instructions, memory traffic, bank conflicts and time spent waiting at barriers), sectors counts the 32-byte pieces of global memory moved, and lines counts lines of code. Par comes from a reference solution. The numbers come from a small made-up cost model, not a real GPU. The cost model, in one page.
